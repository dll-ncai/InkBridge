batch 128 on the Urdu subsets m5_ft_ouhdl_p01 / p10 / p30 (= the LUO-FT subsets) or e0_train (p100), lr 1e-4, warmup 200.

## Headline
- **The Stage 2 'fraction curve' is flat and cannot be read as a low-resource result**: the frozen backbone (M5 joint ms9) was trained on 100 % of OUHD-L
  Urdu, so every Stage 2 run already contains full-data Urdu knowledge. Test AIoU is 0.8361 at p01 and 0.8336 at p100 (seed 1; seed 2 within 0.0006),
  vs the backbone's 0.8207: adapters add +0.0154 [+0.0146, +0.0162]* AIoU at p01 and +0.0129 [+0.0116, +0.0142]* at p100 - the same +0.01-0.02 adapter gain seen in Stage 1,
  independent of how much Urdu the adapter stage sees. The proper low-resource comparator would be a backbone with Urdu left out (LUO); that run was not made.
- Against the Route A curve at matched fraction (paired, test 4,406): p01 +0.0829 [+0.0805, +0.0855]* vs LUO-FT p01 and +0.4893 [+0.4822, +0.4958]* vs E0 from scratch; p100 +0.0270 [+0.0254, +0.0286]* vs LUO-FT p100. These gaps are backbone knowledge, not adapter efficiency (previous bullet).
- Order: Stage 2 tau on Urdu test is 0.8733 (p01) to 0.8606 (p100) vs backbone 0.8616 and E0 ms8 0.8917: p100 - joint -0.0027 [-0.0147, +0.0088], p100 - E0 -0.0248 [-0.0383, -0.0130]*; more Urdu adapter training lowers order slightly (p100 - p01 -0.0130 [-0.0241, -0.0022]*).
- Zero-shot German after Stage 2 (non-regression vs the backbone): UNIPEN-de p01 s1 +0.0057 [+0.0029, +0.0075]*, p100 s1 +0.0039 [-0.0001, +0.0062]; OnHW p01 s0 -0.0014 [-0.0020, -0.0008]* (tau -0.0191 [-0.0301, -0.0085]*). No forgetting of the kind the LUO-FT models show (UNIPEN-de FT p01 0.648): frozen backbone + adapters keep German within 0.006 of the backbone.
- Non-regression on the four frozen LTR scripts after Stage 2 was not measured (the Stage 2 sampling group covers Urdu + German only); Stage 1's non-regression table is in section 1.
- Gate: still uniform after Stage 2 (0.25 per expert incl. 'rtl' on every script, rtl at most 0.285 on CASIA zh for seed 0). The 'rtl' expert is not routed to Urdu; the four experts act as one adapter.
